# Sri Venkatesa Institute of Technology - Student Handbook (Sample Data)

NOTE FOR STUDENTS: this is fictional data written for the lab. It exists so that
you can prove your agent is answering from YOUR documents and not from the
model's training data. No real model has ever seen this file.

## 1. Attendance

A student must maintain a minimum of 75 percent attendance in every course to be
permitted to write the end-semester examination. Students between 65 and 74
percent may apply for condonation by paying a fee of Rs. 500 per course before
the last working day of the semester. Students below 65 percent attendance are
detained and must repeat the course.

Attendance is calculated from the first working day of the semester, not from
the date a student joins.

## 2. Internal Assessment

Internal assessment carries 40 marks, split as follows: two internal tests of 20
marks each, of which the better score is taken (20 marks); one assignment (10
marks); one mini-project or seminar (10 marks). The end-semester examination
carries 60 marks. A student must secure a minimum of 40 percent in internal
assessment and 40 percent in the end-semester examination separately to pass
the course.

## 3. Laboratory Courses

Every laboratory course requires a minimum of 75 percent attendance and
completion of all prescribed experiments. Lab records must be submitted weekly.
A student who misses more than two lab sessions without prior permission will
not be allowed to appear for the lab examination. Internal assessment for labs
is based on continuous evaluation (60 percent) and a practical examination
(40 percent).

## 4. Library

Each student may borrow a maximum of four books at a time for a period of 14
days. Books may be renewed once for another 14 days if no other student has
reserved them. An overdue fine of Rs. 2 per day per book is charged. Reference
books, journals and project reports cannot be borrowed and must be used only
inside the library. Loss of a book requires payment of twice the current price
of the book plus a processing fee of Rs. 100.

## 5. Hostel

Hostel gates close at 9:30 pm on weekdays and 10:00 pm on weekends. Late entry
requires prior written permission from the warden. Visitors are allowed only
between 4:00 pm and 6:00 pm on Sundays and must register at the gate. Cooking
inside rooms is strictly prohibited. A student who damages hostel property will
be charged the full repair cost and may face disciplinary action. Wi-Fi is
available in common areas; personal routers are not permitted.

## 6. Project Work

Final-year project work is compulsory. Students must form teams of 2 to 4
members and submit a project proposal by the end of the seventh semester.
Progress reviews are conducted every month. The final project report must be
submitted two weeks before the project viva. Plagiarism above 20 percent in the
report will result in rejection and a requirement to redo the project.

## 7. Anti-Malpractice

Possession of any written material, electronic device or communication aid
inside the examination hall is treated as malpractice. The student will be
debarred from that examination and may be debarred from subsequent examinations
for one or more semesters as decided by the malpractice committee. Copying from
another student or allowing another student to copy also constitutes
malpractice and carries the same penalty.

## 8. Grievance Redressal

Any student who has a genuine grievance may submit a written complaint to the
class advisor. If not resolved within seven working days, the student may
escalate the matter to the Head of Department and then to the Grievance
Redressal Committee. Anonymous complaints will not be entertained. The
committee will give a written decision within 15 working days of receiving the
complaint.
